---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Resolve common sign-in, invitation, campaign, character, and wiki problems.
---

## My email sign-in link never arrived

Email delivery for magic-link sign-in is not configured yet. Return to the sign-in page and use **Continue with Google**.

## My invitation says I am using the wrong account

Private email invitations only work for the address chosen by the DM. Use **Switch account**, then sign in with that address. A shareable code is not tied to one email.

## A campaign code stopped working

The code may have expired, been revoked, reached its use limit, or been replaced when the DM regenerated the invitation link. Ask the DM to copy the current code from **Campaign → Invites**.

## I cannot change a campaign

Players can view campaign information but only DMs can manage it. If you are a DM, check whether the campaign is archived; archived campaigns are read-only until restored.

## I cannot leave or change a DM's role

Tablekeep will not leave an active campaign without a DM. Promote another member first, or archive the campaign.

## I cannot attach a character

Check that the character is active, the campaign is active, you are a member, and you do not already have an active sheet in that campaign. You may attach only a character you own.

## My sheet cannot be edited

The sheet may be retired or its campaign may be archived. Choose **Return to play** on a retired sheet, or ask a DM to restore the campaign.

## The wiki shows “The rules service did not answer”

Choose **Try again**. The catalog depends on the reference-data service, so a temporary outage can prevent the category from loading. If the catalog loads but shows no matches, shorten the search or clear a filter.
