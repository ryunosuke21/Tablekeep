The owning player and the campaign's DMs share the same editable sheet. Open it from the character page or from **Campaign → Characters**.

![A full character folio with an alias, ancestry, max HP, classes, backgrounds, conditions, inventory, and purse](https://placehold.co/1200x675/2d3f68/ffffff.png?text=Complete+campaign-specific+character+folio)

## Sheet details

Use an optional campaign alias when the character goes by a different name at this table. Record ancestry, maximum HP, and campaign notes. These values stay with this campaign sheet.

## Classes and total level

Add one row per class, with an optional subclass and a level. Reorder rows when presentation matters. The folio calculates total level by adding every class row, which supports multiclass characters.

## Backgrounds

Add one or more background names and optional notes. Names can come from the rules wiki or be written freely for your table.

## Conditions

Add short condition labels such as “Poisoned,” “Blessed,” or a table-specific effect. Clear a condition in one tap when it ends; it can be added again later.

## Inventory

Each item can have a name, quantity, notes, and an **Equipped** state. Removing an item moves it to **Removed gear** instead of erasing it, so you can put it back with its details intact.

## Purse

Currencies are freely named. Track gold, favors, ration tokens, or any economy your game uses. Removing a currency preserves its balance for later restoration.

## Saving and read-only states

Each editor reports whether its change saved or needs retrying. A retired sheet and every sheet in an archived campaign are read-only until returned to play or the campaign is restored.

> The sheet tracks maximum HP, not current HP. Spellbooks and prepared-spell tracking are also not available yet.
