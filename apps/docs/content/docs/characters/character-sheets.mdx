A campaign sheet reads as a character profile. Open it from the character page or from **Campaign → Characters**.

![A character profile with a name plate and tabs for overview, lore, inventory, spellbook, and changes](https://placehold.co/1200x675/2d3f68/ffffff.png?text=Character+profile+with+tabs)

The plate at the top answers who this is before any tab is opened: name, any campaign alias, the campaign, ancestry, classes, alignment, total level, and maximum HP.

## Who can edit

The campaign's DM edits the sheet. Everyone who can open it — including the player who owns the character — sees the same layout in a read-only view.

Editing your own sheet as a player is planned and not available yet. Ask your DM to make a change in the meantime.

A retired sheet, and every sheet in an archived campaign, is read-only for the DM too until it is returned to play or the campaign is restored.

## Overview

Stats, classes, backgrounds, feats, conditions, and description.

Ability scores are freely named, so a table that uses a different spread than the usual six still fits. Classes take one row each, with an optional subclass and a level; total level is the sum of every row, which supports multiclass characters. Backgrounds and feats accept names from the rules wiki or anything written for your table. Conditions are short labels such as “Poisoned” or “Blessed,” cleared in one tap when they end.

The description section holds an optional campaign alias for a character who goes by another name at this table, plus ancestry, alignment, appearance, and campaign notes.

## Lore

The character's backstory for this campaign, their global bio, and the people they know.

Backstory is campaign-specific: the same character can carry a different history at a different table. The bio below it comes from the character's identity and is the same everywhere. Connections record a name, the relationship, and notes — a mentor, a rival, or whoever is still owed a favor.

## Inventory

Each item has a name, quantity, notes, and an **Equipped** state. Removing an item moves it to **Removed gear** instead of erasing it, so it can be put back with its details intact.

Currencies in the purse are freely named. Track gold, favors, ration tokens, or any economy your game uses. Removing a currency preserves its balance for later restoration.

## Spellbook

Every spell the character has learned, grouped by level, with the ones prepared today marked. Cantrips group as level 0. A spell can be added by name from the rules wiki or written freely, and preparing or unpreparing one is a single tap.

Spell state belongs to the campaign sheet, so the same character can carry a different spellbook at a different table.

## Changes

Who changed what on this sheet, and when, most recent first. Each entry names the person, whether they acted as a DM or a player, and what changed.

This history is a record, not an editor: nothing in Tablekeep rewrites or deletes it.

> The sheet tracks maximum HP, not current HP.
