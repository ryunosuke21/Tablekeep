---
title: Retire, delete, and restore characters
description: Pause one campaign sheet or remove a global character without losing its history.
---

Tablekeep offers two different lifecycle actions.

## Retire one campaign sheet

The campaign's DM opens the sheet and chooses **Retire sheet**. The sheet stops appearing among active campaign sheets, but the character identity and sheets in other campaigns remain active.

The DM chooses **Return to play** on the retired sheet to make it editable again, as long as the campaign itself is active.

## Delete the character identity

Open the character's identity page and choose **Delete character**. This removes the character from the Active list and retires all of its campaign sheets together. Its campaign history is preserved.

![A delete-character confirmation explaining that all campaign sheets will retire and the character can be restored](https://placehold.co/1200x675/66354a/ffffff.png?text=Delete+character+and+retire+all+sheets+confirmation)

## Restore a deleted character

Open **Characters** and find **Recently deleted**. Restore the character to return it to the Active list. Its prior campaign records remain associated with it; return individual sheets to play where appropriate.

> Retiring a sheet affects one table. Deleting a character affects the global identity and every table where it has a sheet.
