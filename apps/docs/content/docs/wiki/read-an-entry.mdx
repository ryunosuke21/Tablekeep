Choose any result to open its record page. Pages are arranged like reference articles: identity first, quick numbers together, then detailed sections.

![A creature entry with title chips, a sticky quick-facts infobox, contents list, source link, and article sections](https://placehold.co/1200x675/2f425f/ffffff.png?text=Wiki+entry+with+infobox%2C+contents%2C+and+source)

## Scan the page

- Chips beneath the title identify what the entry is, such as a spell's level and school or a creature's size and challenge rating.
- **At a glance** holds the numbers most useful during play.
- **Contents** links jump to the entry's available sections.
- The article body contains descriptions, traits, features, actions, or other category-specific details.
- The source area identifies the open-reference document and links to its published page when available.

## Dice notation

Recognized expressions such as `1d20 + 5`, `2d6`, or `1d8 - 1` receive a visual die marker. The marker helps the expression stand out in dense rules text; it does not roll the dice.

Supported die sizes are d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, and d20.

## Entry artwork

Some records have hand-added artwork. Records without art use a dimmed category plate, so every entry keeps a consistent layout.
