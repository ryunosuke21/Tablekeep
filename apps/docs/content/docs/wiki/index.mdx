The rules wiki collects every entry made available by Tablekeep's open reference libraries. It is built for quick use on a phone and does not hide entries behind a default source or rules edition.

![The Rules wiki hub listing Species, Backgrounds, Classes, Spells, Creatures, Feats, Items, and Rules](https://placehold.co/1200x675/263a5b/ffffff.png?text=Rules+wiki+category+hub)

## Available categories

- **Species:** playable species and subspecies traits.
- **Backgrounds:** origins, skills, and starting details.
- **Classes:** classes, subclasses, hit dice, and features by level.
- **Spells:** casting details, components, effects, and damage.
- **Creatures:** statistics, traits, actions, and encounter details.
- **Feats:** prerequisites and benefits.
- **Items:** mundane gear and magic items in one catalog.
- **Rules:** reference articles for common game procedures.

## Source notes

Entries come from open reference documents published through Open5e. An entry identifies its source, and its detail page links to the upstream source when available. Tablekeep does not reproduce commercial compendiums or proprietary rulebooks.
