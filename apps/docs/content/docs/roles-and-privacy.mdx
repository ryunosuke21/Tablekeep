---
title: Roles and privacy
description: Understand what players and DMs can see and change.
---

Campaigns are private by default. Joining one gives access to that campaign only, and every protected action is checked by the server.

| Area | Player | DM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign overview and members | View | View |
| Campaign details, images, and schedule | View | Manage |
| Invitations and member roles | No access | Manage |
| Own character identity | Manage | Only if they own it |
| Own campaign sheet | Manage | Co-manage |
| Another player's campaign sheet | No access | Co-manage |
| Rules wiki | View | View |

## Character privacy

A player owns the global character identity. Other players cannot browse that identity or sheet. A campaign's DMs can open and edit the campaign-specific sheet because they co-manage the table's playable state, but they do not take ownership of the global identity.

## Campaign roles are separate

The **Player** and **DM** roles apply inside one campaign. They are separate from any site-wide administrator status. Being a DM in one campaign gives no access to a different campaign.

## Archived and removed access

Archived campaigns remain readable to their members but become read-only. Leaving or being removed ends campaign access immediately. Membership history is retained for the campaign, and a later invitation can add the person again.
