title: Current features
description: See what Tablekeep supports today and what remains planned.
---

This guide documents released behavior. The list below keeps future product direction from being mistaken for something already available.

## Available now

- Google sign-in and first-time profile setup.
- Private campaigns with player and DM roles.
- Campaign profiles, colors, cover images, and icons.
- Email invitations plus expiring, revocable shareable links and codes.
- Member role changes, removals, departures, and last-DM protection.
- Recurring schedules with cancelled, moved, and one-off sessions.
- Campaign archival and restoration.
- Player-owned character identities with campaign-specific sheets, read as a profile with tabs.
- Freely named ability scores, multiclass levels, multiple backgrounds, feats, alignment, appearance, maximum HP, notes, and conditions.
- Campaign backstory and character connections.
- Inventory with equipment state, removed-gear recovery, and freely named currencies.
- Campaign spellbooks with learned spells by level and prepared-spell tracking.
- An append-only record of who changed what on a sheet.
- A searchable, filterable rules wiki for species, backgrounds, classes, spells, creatures, feats, items, and rules.
- List and card wiki views, source attribution, optional entry artwork, and visual dice notation.
- Light, dark, and system appearance preferences.

## Planned, not available yet

- Delivered email magic links for account sign-in.
- Closed-beta admission controls.
- Editing your own campaign sheet as a player. Sheets are currently edited by the campaign's DM.
- Party management beyond the member roster.
- Encounter and initiative tracking.
- Current hit points as shared encounter state.
- Creature encounter preparation, shops, and vendor inventory.
- Digital rolling tools beyond displaying dice notation in wiki text.

> A character sheet is edited by the campaign's DM. Players read their own sheet; a player-facing editor is planned.
