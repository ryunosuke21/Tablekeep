## What Tablekeep is for

Tablekeep supports the social, physical game already happening at your table. It does not try to replace conversation, physical dice, a virtual tabletop, or your rulebooks. Use it for shared campaign organization, player-owned characters, campaign sheets, and quick rules lookups.

> The guides describe released behavior only. See [Current features](./current-features.mdx) for a clean line between what works now and what is still planned.

## The two roles

- **Players** manage their own character identities and campaign sheets. Other players cannot browse those sheets.
- **DMs** manage campaign details, invitations, roles, and schedules. They can also open and co-manage every active character sheet in their campaign.

Role permissions are enforced by the app, not just hidden in the interface. Learn more in [Roles and privacy](./roles-and-privacy.mdx).
