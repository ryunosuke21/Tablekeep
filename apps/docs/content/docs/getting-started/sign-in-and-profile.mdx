## Sign in

Choose **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page and complete the Google prompt. Tablekeep uses the account to keep private campaigns and characters attached to the right person.

![The Tablekeep sign-in screen with Continue with Google as the primary working sign-in option](https://placehold.co/1200x675/1c2548/ffffff.png?text=Tablekeep+sign-in+screen+with+Google+button)

> **Email sign-in status:** the email field is present, but sign-in emails are not delivered yet. Use Google sign-in for now.

## Complete your player profile

New accounts stop at profile setup before entering the rest of the app.

1. Enter your real name, nickname, or usual table name.
2. Optionally select a profile image. Images can be up to **4 MB**.
3. Check the preview showing how you will appear to your party.
4. Choose **Enter Tablekeep**.

Your email remains private and is used for the account. Campaign members see your profile name and picture.

![The player profile setup form with a name field, optional portrait picker, and party-facing profile preview](https://placehold.co/1200x675/4b3f72/ffffff.png?text=Player+profile+name%2C+portrait%2C+and+preview)

## Invitation handoff

Tablekeep remembers where you were going. If you followed a campaign invite and had to sign in or create a profile first, you return to the invitation preview when setup is complete.
