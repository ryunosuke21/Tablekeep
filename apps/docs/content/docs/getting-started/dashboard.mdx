The dashboard summarizes the campaigns you run or play in and the characters you own. Campaign cards show the next scheduled session when one is available.

![The Tablekeep dashboard with campaign and character sections plus the expanded left sidebar](https://placehold.co/1200x675/22315a/f7f7ff.png?text=Dashboard+with+campaigns%2C+characters%2C+and+sidebar)

## Main navigation

The sidebar has two groups:

- **Personal:** Campaigns and Characters.
- **Wiki:** Species, Backgrounds, Classes, Spells, Creatures, Feats, Items, and Rules.

On a narrow screen, open the navigation drawer to reach the same destinations. The sidebar can collapse to icons on a larger screen.

## Create something new

Open **Create new** near the top of the sidebar to start a campaign or character. You can also use **New campaign** on the Campaigns page and **Create a character** on the Characters page.

## Appearance, help, and feedback

Open your profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar to choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** appearance and to sign out. Use **Help** to open this guide. Use **Feedback** to send feedback from inside the app.
