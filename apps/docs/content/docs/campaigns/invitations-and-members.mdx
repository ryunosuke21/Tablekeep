---
title: Invitations and members
description: Invite players or DMs, manage roles, and remove access safely.
---

Campaign invitations and member management are DM-only tasks.

## Invite someone

Open the campaign's **Invites** page and choose **Invite people**.

### Private email invitation

Enter an email address, choose whether the person joins as a **Player** or **DM**, and choose **Send invitation**. Only that address can accept it. Pending invitations can be resent or revoked.

### Shareable link and code

Create an invitation link, then copy the full link or the human-enterable code. Anyone with the active code can join with its assigned role until it expires or is revoked.

![The Invite people dialog showing a private email invitation and a shareable link with copy, regenerate, and revoke actions](https://placehold.co/1200x675/322f5f/ffffff.png?text=Email+invite+and+shareable+campaign+code)

Regenerating the link replaces the code immediately. Revoking it stops new joins but does not remove existing members.

## Manage the roster

Open **Members** to see names, roles, and join dates. A DM can:

- Promote a player to DM.
- Change another DM to player.
- Remove another member.

Players can view the roster but cannot change someone else's role or access. A removed member loses campaign access immediately and can be invited again later.

## Protect the last DM

Every active campaign must keep a DM. Tablekeep prevents the last DM from being demoted, removed, or leaving. Promote another member first, or archive the campaign if the table is finished.
