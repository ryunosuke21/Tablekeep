---
title: Archive, leave, and restore
description: Close a campaign without deleting its history, or remove your own access.
---

## Archive a campaign

A DM can open **Settings → Archive** and confirm the action. Archiving:

- Preserves campaign records.
- Makes the campaign read-only.
- Revokes pending invitations.
- Prevents new members and character sheets.
- Moves the campaign to the **Archived** filter on the Campaigns page.

![The campaign Settings page with an archive warning and a confirmation dialog describing the read-only result](https://placehold.co/1200x675/5b3444/ffffff.png?text=Archive+campaign+confirmation)

## Restore a campaign

Open **Campaigns**, switch to **Archived**, open the campaign, and restore it from **Settings**. The campaign becomes editable again. Old invitations remain revoked, so create new ones when needed.

## Leave a campaign

Use **Your membership** in campaign settings or the action beside your name on the Members page. Leaving removes your access but does not delete the campaign.

The last DM cannot leave an active campaign. Promote another member first or archive the campaign.
