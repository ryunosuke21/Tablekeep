## Create the campaign

1. Open **Campaigns**.
2. Choose **New campaign**.
3. Enter a campaign name and, optionally, a short description.
4. Choose the campaign colors.
5. Optionally add a cover image and icon.
6. Choose **Create campaign**.

The creator becomes the campaign's first DM. Tablekeep also gives the campaign its own URL; changing the campaign name later does not change that existing URL.

![The new campaign form with name, description, color controls, cover image, icon, and a live campaign-card preview](https://placehold.co/1200x675/33406a/ffffff.png?text=New+campaign+form+with+live+card+preview)

## Change the campaign profile

Open the campaign and choose **Settings**. DMs can update:

- **Details:** name, description, and colors.
- **Campaign images:** cover image and icon.
- **Schedule:** the regular meeting pattern.

Everyone in the campaign sees the updated profile on its card and overview.

> Only a campaign DM can open Settings or change campaign-owned information.
