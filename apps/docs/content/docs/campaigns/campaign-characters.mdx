Open a campaign's **Characters** section to work with the character sheets used at that table.

## For players

You can attach one of your active characters when you do not already have a sheet in that campaign. Select the character and choose **Bring them to the table**. If you have no character yet, create one first.

Other players cannot browse your sheet. You and the campaign's DMs can open and edit the same campaign-specific sheet.

## For DMs

DMs see every active sheet in the campaign and can co-manage it with the owning player. A DM may also attach one of their own characters if they want to play at the same table.

![The campaign Characters page showing all sheets to a DM and an attach-character control below](https://placehold.co/1200x675/3d456f/ffffff.png?text=Campaign+character+roster+and+attach+control)

## One identity, separate sheets

The character's name and bio belong to the player and follow the character everywhere. Each campaign keeps its own alias, ancestry, maximum HP, notes, classes, backgrounds, conditions, inventory, and currencies. Changes at one table do not rewrite the sheet at another.
