A campaign is the private home for one table. It has a profile, member roster, roles, invitations, recurring schedule, upcoming sessions, and campaign-specific character sheets.

## Campaign sections

- **Overview** shows the schedule, upcoming sessions, and roster preview.
- **Characters** holds the sheets used at this table.
- **Members** shows everyone in the campaign and their role.
- **Invites** is available to DMs for email invitations and a shareable code.
- **Settings** is available to DMs for details, images, schedule, and archival.

An archived campaign remains readable, but changes stop until a DM restores it.
